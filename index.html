About

Hi, I’m Adi. I’m a photographer, writer, and creative from Kariega, South Africa. This is the home of Tiffany Studios’ Creative Portfolio.

I believe every person has a story worth telling. Whether I’m photographing a family, creating conceptual portraits, or writing poetry, I’m drawn to the quiet moments and emotions that often go unnoticed.

I’ve always been collecting stories. Sometimes they become photographs. Sometimes they become poems. Sometimes they stay tucked away in a notebook until I’m ready to share them.
